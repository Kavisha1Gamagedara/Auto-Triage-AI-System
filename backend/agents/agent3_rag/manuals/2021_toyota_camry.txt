[Vehicle: 2021 Toyota Camry]
[Source: Public DIY video/guides for the Toyota A25A 2.5L engine (e.g. youcanic.com A25A/M20A spark plug guide); torque is a community-reported value - NOT an OEM figure]
Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (2.5L A25A-FKS):
1. Let the engine cool and confirm the correct plug part number for the vehicle by VIN.
2. Remove the engine cover.
3. Disconnect each ignition coil connector (press the clip and pull) and remove the coil bolt with a 10mm socket, then pull the coil.
4. Clean debris from each spark plug well, for example with compressed air, before removing the plug.
5. Remove the old plug with a thin-wall spark plug socket and extension.
6. Hand-thread the new plug. Do not adjust the gap on iridium plugs; the gap is preset.
7. Tighten with a torque wrench, refit the coil and its bolt, and reconnect the connector. Work one cylinder at a time.
8. Reinstall the engine cover and check for misfires.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED against Toyota repair information. A DIY video reports 15 ft-lb (about 20 N.m) for the plugs and 5.5 ft-lb (about 7.5 N.m) for the 8mm coil bolts. Confirm in Toyota TIS before use.
