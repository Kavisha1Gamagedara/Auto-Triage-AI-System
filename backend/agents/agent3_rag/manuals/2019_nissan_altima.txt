Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (2.5L, general outline):
1. Let the engine cool and remove the plastic engine cover.
2. Remove the ignition coils.
3. Use a spark plug socket with an extension to remove the old plugs.
4. Do not re-gap platinum or iridium plugs. Hand-start the new plug.
5. Tighten with a torque wrench, then refit the coils and cover.
Interval: Nissan lists spark plug replacement at 105,000 miles for the 2.5L.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. A 2014 ALLDATA excerpt in a forum post lists 19.6 to 29.4 N.m (15 to 21 ft-lb) for an older Altima. Confirm in the Nissan service manual for the QR25 engine before use.

Component: Mass Air Flow (MAF) Sensor
Replacement Procedure:
1. Turn the ignition off and disconnect the negative battery terminal.
2. Disconnect the single electrical connector on the MAF sensor.
3. Remove the two Phillips head screws holding the probe-style sensor to the intake duct.
4. Pull the sensor straight out of the duct. Do not touch the sensing element.
5. Install the new sensor, secure the two screws snugly, and reconnect the connector.
6. Clear codes and road test. Confirm the part number for your engine (2.5L or 3.5L).
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. No torque value was found; tighten snug only, since overtightening can crack the plastic housing. Confirm in the Nissan service manual.
