Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (5.3L V8, general outline):
1. Disconnect the negative battery terminal before working on the ignition system.
2. Disconnect each ignition coil electrical connector, unbolt the coil if required, and pull it out.
3. Clean the plug well with compressed air, then remove the old plug with a spark plug socket and extension without damaging the cylinder head threads.
4. Check the gap (about 0.040 in for the ACDelco iridium plug cited by one guide). Do not grease the threads unless the service manual says to.
5. Hand-start the new plug and tighten with a torque wrench.
6. Reinstall the coils and connectors.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. A forum post for a 2016 Silverado 5.3L reports 15 to 16 lb-ft; no GM figure for the 2019 truck was found. Confirm in GM service information before use.

[Source: Jet Performance install write-up for 2019-2024 Silverado/Sierra 1500 V8 (gm-efi.com) - NOT GM service information]
Component: Mass Air Flow (MAF) Sensor
Replacement Procedure (V8):
1. Disconnect the negative battery cable (10mm socket).
2. Release the MAF connector: slide the red locking tab to unlocked, press the button on the connector body and pull it off the sensor.
3. Remove the two Allen screws holding the sensor to the inlet ducting. Check the fastener type on your truck.
4. Slip the sensor out of the ducting. Do not touch the sensing element.
5. Install the new sensor by reversing the removal steps.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. No torque value was found for the MAF screws; tighten only snug as they thread into plastic ducting. Confirm in GM service information.
