Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (1.5L turbo):
1. Make sure the engine and cylinder head are completely cold.
2. Remove the engine cover by taking out its top bolts.
3. Unclip the wire harness holder and disconnect the harness from the ignition coil connector.
4. Remove the bolt beside the coil and pull the ignition coil out.
5. Clean debris and moisture out of each spark plug well before removing the plug.
6. Use a spark plug socket on an extension to loosen and remove the old plug.
7. Check the new plug is dry and correctly gapped, then start it by hand to avoid cross-threading.
8. Tighten with a torque wrench. Do not add anti-seize unless the service information says to.
9. Repeat for the other three cylinders, then reinstall the coils, reconnect the connectors and refit the engine cover.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED against the Honda service manual. NGK data for aluminum cylinder heads lists 10.8 to 14.5 lb-ft (about 14.6 to 19.7 N.m). Confirm the exact value in Honda service information before use.

[Source: Go-Parts guide for 2017-2024 Honda Civic oxygen sensors (go-parts.com/garage/ps-2017-2024-honda-civic-oxygen-sensor) and YouCanic Honda Civic O2 sensor guide, paraphrased; NOT Honda service information]
Component: Heated Oxygen (O2) Sensor Replacement
Replacement Procedure (upstream Sensor 1 and downstream Sensor 2):
1. Park on level ground and let the engine cool. Confirm whether the upstream (before the catalytic converter) or downstream (after it) sensor is needed; they are not interchangeable.
2. The upstream sensor is reached from under the open hood; the downstream sensor requires jacking up and supporting the vehicle.
3. Disconnect the sensor's wire harness connector.
4. Unscrew the sensor with a slotted oxygen sensor socket. If it will not turn, spray penetrating oil on the threads and let it soak.
5. Install the new sensor, preferably an OEM-brand part such as Denso or NTK, and reconnect the harness.
6. Clear the codes and confirm the check engine light stays off. The guide rates the job beginner-friendly at about 45 minutes.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. No sensor torque was found for the Civic. Look up the value in Honda service information before use.
