[Vehicle: 2018 Toyota Corolla]
[Source: 2018 Toyota Corolla Service Manual, Section EM-24, Page 118]
Component: Mass Air Flow (MAF) Sensor
Replacement Procedure:
1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect negative battery cable.
2. Disconnect the MAF sensor electrical connector.
3. Remove the 2 retaining screws holding the sensor to the air intake box.
4. Carefully pull the MAF sensor out. Ensure the old O-ring is removed.
5. Install new sensor with a new O-ring.
6. Torque the 2 retaining screws to 8.5 N.m (75 in.lbf).

[Vehicle: 2018 Toyota Corolla]
[Source: Project sample steps; torque from third-party data for the 2ZR-FE 1.8L (2013 Corolla data page, PriusChat thread) - NOT Toyota repair information]
Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure:
1. Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
2. Disconnect the electrical connector from the ignition coil.
3. Remove the 8mm coil mounting bolt and pull the ignition coil straight up.
4. Use a 5/8 inch spark plug socket to carefully remove the old spark plug.
5. Hand-start the new spark plug, then tighten it with a torque wrench.
6. Reinstall the ignition coil and its mounting bolt.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED against Toyota repair information. Third-party data for the 1.8L 2ZR-FE lists 20 N.m (about 14.8 lb-ft), gap 1.1 mm; another source reports 13 ft-lb (about 18 N.m). The earlier sample value of 11 ft-lbs was below all sources found. Confirm in Toyota TIS before use.

[Vehicle: 2018 Toyota Corolla]
[Source: Project sample data (unverified against OEM manual)]
Component: Catalytic Converter
Replacement Procedure:
1. Raise and support the vehicle safely on jack stands.
2. Spray penetrating oil on the exhaust flange bolts and let sit.
3. Disconnect the upstream and downstream Oxygen (O2) sensors.
4. Remove the 14mm bolts connecting the catalytic converter to the exhaust manifold.
5. Remove the rear flange bolts and carefully lower the old converter.
6. Install the new catalytic converter with new gaskets and torque flange bolts to 32 ft-lbs (43 N.m).
