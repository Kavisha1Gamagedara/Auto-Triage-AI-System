Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (general outline):
1. Wait for a cold engine and clean the spark plug wells with compressed air before removing anything.
2. Disconnect each ignition coil connector and remove the coil.
3. Remove the old plug with a spark plug wrench. Never install a plug that has been dropped.
4. Check the plug gap, hand-tighten the new plug, then tighten with a torque wrench to specification.
5. Reinstall the coils and connectors.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. No torque value for the 2018 CR-V 1.5T was found. Do not use figures from other Honda engines. Look up the value in Honda service information before use.
