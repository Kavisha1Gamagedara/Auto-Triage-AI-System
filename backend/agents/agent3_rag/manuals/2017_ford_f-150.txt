[Vehicle: 2017 Ford F-150]
[Source: Owner forum and aftermarket spark plug guides for the 3.5L EcoBoost (f150gen14.com spark plug thread; seatcoversolutions.com F-150 spark plug guide) - NOT Ford service information]
Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (3.5L EcoBoost, general outline):
1. Let the engine cool completely.
2. Remove the engine cover and any intake parts that block access to the coil packs.
3. Disconnect each ignition coil connector and remove the coil.
4. Remove the old plug with a spark plug socket and extension. Note the electrode orientation of the old plug.
5. Check the new plug gap (pre-gapped cold plugs are listed at 0.031 in / 0.8 mm; confirm against the underhood emissions sticker).
6. Hand-start the new plug and tighten with a calibrated torque wrench.
7. Refit the coils, connectors and covers.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. An owner of a newer F-150 with the 3.5L family reports 18 lb-ft (about 24 N.m). No source for the 2017 truck was found. Confirm in the Ford workshop manual before use.
