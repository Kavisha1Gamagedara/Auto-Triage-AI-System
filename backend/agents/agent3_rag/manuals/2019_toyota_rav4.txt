Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (2.5L, general outline):
1. Let the engine cool. Confirm the engine code (non-hybrid A25A-FKS, hybrid A25A-FXS).
2. Remove the engine cover and the four ignition coils (10mm bolts), working one cylinder at a time.
3. Clean each plug well, then remove the old plug with a 14mm thin-wall spark plug socket and a long extension.
4. Hand-start the new plug. Do not adjust the gap on pre-gapped iridium plugs.
5. Tighten with a torque wrench, refit the coil, then move to the next cylinder.
6. If the engine misfires afterward, recheck coil connectors, plug seating and OBD-II codes.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED against Toyota repair information. A third-party guide reports 20 N.m (about 15 ft-lb). Confirm in Toyota TIS before use.
