[Vehicle: 2019 Hyundai Elantra]
[Source: YouCanic "Change spark plugs Hyundai Kia four-cylinder engine" guide (Theta II engines); engine family for the 2019 Elantra 2.0L is not confirmed - NOT Hyundai service information]
Component: Spark Plug
Replacement Procedure (2.0L, general outline):
1. Park, set the parking brake and let the engine cool. Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
2. The engine uses coil-on-plug ignition: unplug the coil connector, remove the 10mm bolt and lift the coil out, twisting slightly if it sticks.
3. Blow debris away from the plug recess before removing the plug.
4. Remove the plug with a spark plug socket and extension; a magnetic pickup tool can retrieve a stuck plug.
5. Install the new plug and tighten with a torque wrench, not by feel.
6. Reinstall the coils and connectors.
Torque: NOT VERIFIED. The guide lists 11 to 15 lb-ft for plugs and 3.9 to 5.9 N.m (2.9 to 4.3 lb-ft) for coil bolts on Theta II engines and says to verify with a dealer. Confirm the engine code and the value in Hyundai service information before use.
